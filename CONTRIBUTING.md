# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome, and they get judged against
[docs/PHILOSOPHY.md](docs/PHILOSOPHY.md). **Read that first** — it is short,
and it is the whole of what this app is trying to be.

A feature can be good and still be wrong for this app. Most of the rules in
there exist to say no to something, and "another tracker has it" is not a
reason. If you are not sure a change fits, open an issue before you write it.
